# Interview Notes

### What problem did you solve?
I explored whether hidden vendor characteristics could be inferred from
observable marketplace signals to support vendor prioritisation and matching.

### Why latent variables?
The business concepts I cared about, such as reliability and commercial
potential, were not directly observable as one field.

### Why Factor Analysis?
It provides a transparent way to estimate lower-dimensional latent structure
and inspect factor loadings.

### Why clustering?
It turns continuous latent scores into interpretable vendor segments.

### Why semantic matching?
Numeric vendor metrics do not capture the semantic relationship between a
business requirement and a vendor/product description.

### What would you do in production?
Replace synthetic data with governed marketplace data, define ground-truth
outcomes, add human feedback, monitor drift, and measure business acceptance,
latency and cost.
